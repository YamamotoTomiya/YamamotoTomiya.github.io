I am Tomiya Yamamoto, a first year Ph.D. student in the Division of Materials Science at the Nara Institute of Science and Technology ([NAIST](https://www.naist.jp/en/)), Japan, in the [Materials Informatics Laboratory](https://sites.google.com/view/naist-mi) led by Prof. Mikiya Fujii. My research lies at the intersection of materials science and artificial intelligence, with a particular focus on developing generative models to accelerate the discovery of novel inorganic materials.

Currently, I am conducting a research internship at Nanyang Technological University ([NTU](https://www.ntu.edu.sg/)), Singapore, in [the HIP Lab](https://kedarh.wixsite.com/nanotransport) led by Assoc. Prof. Kedar Hippalgaonkar, where I work on designing AI-driven approaches for crystal structure generation. Previously, I joined the [AiMat group](https://aimat.iti.kit.edu/people.php?tab=%5B361%5D#tabpanel-361) at the Karlsruhe Institute of Technology ([KIT](https://www.kit.edu/english/)), Germany, under the supervision of T.T.-Prof. Pascal Friederich, where I explored the use of large language models (LLMs) for extracting synthesis-related knowledge from scientific literature.
